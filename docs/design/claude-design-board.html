AniReel · Claude design draft

Your feed is the camera roll. The camera is one tap from anywhere.

A competing design board for the D9 restructure. Works come first, the camera button sits in the middle of every screen, and the only bold colour is reserved for the moment you shoot. Aurora cyan → blue → violet carries the brand; coral marks the action. Gender-neutral, Apple-minimal, built for 15–28.

Tokens

One gradient for the brand (the duotone A), one for surfaces of identity (aurora), and one warm accent for doing things (coral). Everything else is ink on white.

Five layout decisions in this draft

These are where it intentionally differs from a straight transcription of the brief.

1 · Floating capsule tab barThe bar floats over the feed as one glass capsule with the camera docked in the middle, so video runs edge to edge. It turns white on light tabs.
2 · Vertical reel indicatorA thin segmented strip on the right edge of the feed shows where you are in your works, like Stories turned sideways.
3 · Samples feel different from worksSample pages use a coral corner ribbon and a glass card with a CTA. Real works use the minimal Douyin overlay. Nobody mistakes an illustration for their own clip.
4 · Quota stays visible but quietA chip at the top of the feed, a meter on the registration wall, and a stat in the profile card. It's never a modal until the shutter is pressed.
5 · Profile is works-first tooThe clips grid sits right under the identity card. Settings and legal drop to a short list at the bottom.

The scene art is illustrative SVG made for this board, not footage. Copy is shown in Simplified Chinese, the primary market; every string has an English counterpart in the app. Rationale is in docs/design/CLAUDE_DESIGN_NOTES.md.
